## [1.0.0] — August 2026

### Added
- Initial release of the **7-agent agentic pipeline**:
  - `SpecAgentAI`, `CodegenAgentAI`, `CritiqueAgentAI`, `RedTeamAgentAI`, `TestAgentAI`, `MonitorAgentAI`, `RepairAgentAI`

- W3C html5lib compliance test runner (bundled sample `.dat` files)
